Key Insights

Career Arc: Started at 21 with an impressive 5th place. Steady improvement through his 20s, peaking at 30 with the 27:14 PR (16:15/mi). Since then, times have stabilized in the 30-31h range at ages 35-37.

Direction Matters: Every CCW finish was faster than the nearest CW finish by 1-3 hours. Part of this is the course profile — the CCW direction tackles the steepest climbs earlier when legs are fresher.

Aid Station Discipline: The fastest year (2017) spent only 35 minutes in aid stations across the entire 100 miles. The slowest (2016) spent 90 minutes. Quick transitions are a clear performance indicator.

Consistency: Three of the last four finishes (2022, 2024 CW; 2015 CCW) clustered tightly around 30:25-30:37 — remarkably consistent for a 100-mile mountain race with varying conditions.

Youthful Start: Nick's first Hardrock at age 21 (2008) resulted in 5th place and 31:07 — one of the strongest debuts in race history for that age group.
